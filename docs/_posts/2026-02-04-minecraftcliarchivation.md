So for those that do not know, about a year ago I was crazy into TUI apps made with Bubbletea and the Go language. Because of that, I spent like a week trying to make a minecraft launcher for the command line. However, my procrastrination had eventually made me lose interest over time and now I pretty much can't code in Golang anymore (mostly because of weird syntax quirks). After 11 months of no commits, I'm officially archiving the repositories on GitHub.

Here's what you can do if you still want it:
* Make it yourself but remember to credit me
* Try out [PandoraLauncher](https://github.com/Moulberry/PandoraLauncher) instead
